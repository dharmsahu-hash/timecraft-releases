# TimeCraft privacy policy

*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

TimeCraft is a free time and focus tracker made by [KnowledgeWala](https://knowledgewala.com) (developer: Dharmendra Sahu, contact: dknitk@gmail.com). This policy covers the TimeCraft app for Android (`com.knowledgewala.timecraft`).

## The short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your topics, sessions, targets and settings stay on your phone. We do not receive them and we run no server.
- The optional features that use the internet are off until you turn them on (see below).

## What the app stores on your phone

Topics you create, the time sessions you record, your targets, your daily goal and your settings. They are kept in the app's private storage. If you choose AI insights, your AI key is stored in Android's encrypted storage on the phone. You can delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Android's own backup (if you have it switched on in your phone settings) may copy the app's data to your Google account, under Google's terms. The AI key is excluded from that backup. The in-app **Back up now** button writes a file only where you choose to save it.

## Optional: AI insights (off by default)

If you enter an AI provider address and your own API key in Settings, the app sends that provider a short summary to write an insight: the period or target name, topic names, and totals in hours. It does not send your individual session times. The text goes straight from your phone to the provider you chose, under their privacy policy; we never see it and we do not keep a copy of questions or answers. The app only counts how many AI calls were made (shown in **Your data & usage**).

## Optional: update check (off by default; not in the Google Play version)

The version downloaded from our GitHub page has an update check; the Google Play version does not (Google Play updates it). In the GitHub version, if you switch on **Check for updates once a day** in About, the app asks the public GitHub releases page of TimeCraft (`api.github.com`) which version is newest. It sends nothing about you or your data (GitHub receives the request as it would from any app, including your IP address). It never downloads or installs anything by itself.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to show the running timer and the idle reminder.
- **Foreground service and start at boot**: to keep a running timer alive while the app is in the background and to restore it after a restart.
- **Internet**: only for the optional features above (AI insights; update check in the GitHub version).

## Children

TimeCraft does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
